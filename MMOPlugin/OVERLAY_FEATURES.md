# MMO Overlays – Product Features

This overview is based on the locally available **`MMOverlays_v7.0.4.zip`** package. It describes the features visible in the dashboard layouts and their included configuration. Individual overlays may have different version numbers inside the package; for example, the package contains LapInfo **v7.0.2**, Leaderboard **v7.0.4**, several overlays **v7.0.1**, and Teamspeak **v7.0.0**.

## MMO – LapInfo

- Keep your key lap data in one compact view: last lap, personal best, current lap, and predicted lap time.
- Follow your live pace against the session best with an easy-to-read delta.
- Quickly spot a personal best or the fastest lap in your class through color highlights.
- Lap times adapt to pit-lane and unavailable-time situations, so the display stays readable when no valid lap time is available.

## MMO – Leaderboard

- Follow the field around your current position, with a flexible number of visible cars.
- See each driver's position, name, best lap, current lap, gap to the leader, and interval to the next car.
- Spot the player, class leaders, and standout lap times at a glance.
- See whether cars are in the pits, in the garage, on an out lap, or finished.
- Compare tyre compounds across the field, with display options for all cars, your class, or only cars on a different compound.
- Show a driver's latest lap as a temporary popup.
- Follow start lights and session countdown cues, with layouts that adapt to race, practice, and qualifying sessions.

## MMO – MiniCarData

- A compact at-a-glance view of your car, including gear, revs, last lap, and live lap delta.
- Keep key setup and driver-assist values close, including ABS, traction control, brake bias, engine map, and anti-roll bars.
- Monitor battery charge where available.
- See fuel or Virtual Energy (VE), depending on your car, together with the estimated laps remaining.
- Low fuel or energy reserves are highlighted so you can react before they become critical.

## MMO – Notification Center

- Get clear, compact alerts for fuel, VE, tyres, engine state, pit limiter, and driver aids.
- See when fuel or energy is running low or depleted, with the more urgent reserve called out.
- Monitor tyre pressure and wear, with thresholds that account for wet tyres and pit-stop conditions.
- Keep an eye on battery charge and receive visual cues when it is very low or high.
- Surface changes to ABS, traction control, brake bias, engine map, and other car settings without needing to search through menus.
- Show ignition and engine state, pit-limiter status, and speed-related warnings.

## MMO – Relative

- Keep nearby cars in view with relative gaps, positions, names, and class information for cars ahead and behind.
- Recognize cars in the pits, garage, or on an out lap, and follow their race status.
- Compare tyre compounds and view temporary last-lap popups.
- Track your own fuel and VE use, estimated laps remaining, and the energy or refuelling amount needed to finish.
- Includes supporting race and environment information such as track temperature, rain, wetness, and PC time.
- Provides a race fuel estimate for both lap-limited and timed races, using the remaining race distance/time and your current fuel use.

## MMO – Rev Bar

- A clear rev bar with progressive shift-light colors to help you choose the right moment to shift.
- Keep gear, last lap, predicted lap time, and live delta in view while driving.
- Monitor fuel or VE and the estimated laps remaining for your car.
- Low reserves are highlighted before they become critical.
- Includes useful cockpit cues such as ABS and traction-control activity, ignition, lights, and hybrid/lift-and-coast status where available.

## MMO – SectorBox

- Follow the current sector time alongside your own and the best sector comparison.
- The displayed sector time updates as you progress around the lap.
- Designed to focus on the player's class.

## MMO – Teamspeak

- See your connected TeamSpeak server, current channel, and number of people in the channel.
- View connected clients by name, with clear microphone-muted and speaker-muted indicators.
- Speaking users are highlighted, making it easy to see who is talking during a race.

## MMO – TireInfoHor

- A horizontal tyre view covering all four wheels.
- Check inner, middle, and outer tyre temperatures, tyre wear, pressure, and brake temperature.
- See each tyre's compound with color cues, including wet-weather tyres.
- Includes a combined tyre-temperature reading for a quick overall check.

## MMO – TireInfoVer

- A vertical layout of the same four-wheel tyre information for dashboards with a taller, narrower space.
- Check inner, middle, and outer tyre temperatures, tyre wear, pressure, and brake temperature for each wheel.
- See tyre compounds and wet-weather cues, plus a combined temperature reading.

## MMO – TrackMap

- A dedicated track-map dashboard that appears while a game session is active.
- Designed to provide a focused map view alongside the other MMO displays.
